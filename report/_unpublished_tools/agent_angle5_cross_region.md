# Cross-Region Cadre Exchange Network — 拉萨市 (Lhasa City)

**Research Angle 5 of 5 — Cross-County/City Exchange Network**
**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Researcher**: Autonomous investigation agent

---

## Executive Summary

拉萨市 (Lhasa), as the capital of Tibet Autonomous Region, sits at the center of a unique two-tier cadre exchange system:
1. **对口支援 (Pairing Assistance)** — Provinces like Beijing and Jiangsu send cadres to work in Lhasa
2. **Intra-Tibet rotation** — Cadres move between Lhasa and other prefectures (日喀则, 林芝, 山南, etc.)
3. **Central government ties** — Some cadres originate from national ministries (农业部, 共青团中央)

This document maps ALL confirmed cross-region connections found in current Lhasa leadership.

**Observation**: Lhasa is UNIQUE in receiving from **two provinces simultaneously** — both 北京 and 江苏. Elsewhere in Tibet, one prefecture gets one province:
- 山南 → 湖南/湖北
- 林芝 → 福建/广东
- 昌都 → 重庆/天津 (this is the rule)
- **拉萨 → 北京+江苏** (exception!)

### PER Capita 援藏 intensity

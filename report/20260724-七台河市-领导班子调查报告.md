## Governance Context

七台河市 is a resource-based city in eastern Heilongjiang, known as one of the "Four Coal Cities" (四大煤城) of the province. It is a nationally designated resource-exhausted city (第二批资源枯竭城市). The city is also famous as the "Winter Olympic Champion City" (冬奥冠军之城) for its short-track speed skating achievements (杨扬, 王濛, etc.).

Key governance themes:
- Economic transformation from coal dependence
- Winter sports/short-track speed skating legacy development
- "Five Cities One Modernization" (五城一化) development strategy
